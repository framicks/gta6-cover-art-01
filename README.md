Vice Cover Studio — turn your own photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art: a multi-panel Vice City sunset poster with a slatted retro sun, palm silhouettes, a neon colour grade and huge condensed caption lettering, exported as a real PNG to share. Load up to three of your own photos (they never leave your machine), fill several panels with one photo or several, click a panel to crop it — drag to move, scroll to zoom — pick sunset, neon or night grade, and export in 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080. Everything is original art and type: no Rockstar logo, artwork or font; the display type is Noto Sans Condensed Black under the SIL Open Font License. Static files, no build step, no network requests — it works fully offline.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Add photos (or try the samples), click a panel to select it, drag inside it to crop, type your caption, and hit an export button to download the PNG.
